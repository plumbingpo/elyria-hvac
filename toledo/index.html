How Toledo Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Akron

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
